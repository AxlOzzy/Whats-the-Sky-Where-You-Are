Quick Start Guide
01 Enter your location — city, state, country — in the Location ID field.
02 Point your camera at an unobstructed view of the sky.
03 Allow camera access when prompted. Images are never stored or sent — only the hex colour code, your location name, and a timestamp leave your device.
04 Tap Start. Your sky colour will be sampled and sent to the hub live at the selected sample rate — 10 seconds is recommended.
05 Keep this page open and do not let your device sleep. If the page is not active, sampling will stop. We recommend keeping your device plugged in with screen brightness turned down — and checking your settings for any auto-lock or auto-sleep.
